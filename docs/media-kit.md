## Suggested gallery order and captions

1. **One connected export workflow** — Reuse account-owned Company, Buyer, and Product information.
2. **Start with Export Wizard** — Generate an editable Invoice, Packing List, Shipping Instruction, and Shipment chain.
3. **Track the shipment** — Maintain status, dates, container details, and transport context.
4. **See the whole package** — Open completed documents and identify what is still missing.
5. **Review before sharing** — Edit generated documents, create PDFs, and send through the configured email workflow.

## Usage notes

- Keep the product name as “Trade Paper AI”.
- Do not add customer logos or testimonials until a real customer has approved publication.
- Do not publish Starter or Professional prices until they are confirmed.
- Do not imply that external payment processing is active.
- The GIF duration is 15 seconds and uses five three-second frames from the product demo.

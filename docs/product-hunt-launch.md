# Trade Paper AI — Product Hunt Launch

## Product name

Trade Paper AI

## Tagline

Create and manage export documents in one connected workflow.

## Short description

Trade Paper AI is an export operations workspace that helps exporters create, connect, manage, package, and send trade documents without repeatedly entering the same company, buyer, product, and shipment information.

## Long description

Export teams often copy the same commercial, buyer, product, cargo, and shipment details between disconnected documents. That duplicate work is slow, hard to review, and creates avoidable inconsistencies. Trade Paper AI keeps the document chain connected while preserving the saved snapshot of each existing document.

The current Founding Beta supports Quotations, Proforma Invoices, Commercial Invoices, Packing Lists, Shipping Instructions, Shipments, Booking Confirmations, Bills of Lading, Container records, Customs Declarations, Certificates of Origin, Inspection Certificates, Insurance Certificates, and Weight Certificates. Documents can be opened, edited, exported as PDF where supported, searched, archived, and grouped by shipment in a Document Package.

Export Wizard starts a new workflow from reusable Company, Buyer, and Product master data. Choose the core trade details and Trade Paper AI creates an editable Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Shipping Instruction, and Shipment chain. Buyer-specific defaults are suggestions only, so the exporter remains in control of every value.

Email Delivery prepares the supported document PDF—or a Document Package ZIP—for delivery through the configured SMTP backend. The recipient, subject, and professional message template remain editable before sending, and the Shipment workspace records the delivery result without storing message bodies or attachment contents in its history.

Shipment Tracking keeps the current status, container and seal details, ETD, ETA, actual departure and arrival dates, and a tracking memo together. Document Package shows completed and missing documents and provides direct View, Edit, and PDF actions.

Team Workspace is available on the Professional plan structure and supports Owner, Admin, Manager, Staff, and Viewer roles. Account isolation, role checks, audit logging, backup and restore, and email security controls apply to the implemented workspace.

Trade Paper AI is currently recruiting its first Founding Beta companies. There are no published customer testimonials or customer logos yet. Founding Beta applicants receive direct onboarding and priority support, and the team will contact applicants within two business days.

## FAQ

### Who is it for?

Exporters and export operations teams that prepare and manage recurring trade documents and want one connected workflow from buyer and product data through shipment delivery.

### Is there a free plan?

Yes. The current Free plan supports up to five documents per month. Starter and Professional plan structures are implemented, but external payment processing is not active yet and unconfirmed prices are not published.

### Which export documents are supported?

The Founding Beta supports Quotations, Proforma and Commercial Invoices, Packing Lists, Shipping Instructions, Shipments, Booking Confirmations, Bills of Lading, Container records, Customs Declarations, Certificates of Origin, Inspection Certificates, Insurance Certificates, and Weight Certificates. PDF availability follows the currently implemented document workflow.

### Can my team use it?

Yes. The Professional plan structure supports invitations and Owner, Admin, Manager, Staff, and Viewer roles. Each team operates inside its account-owned workspace.

## Maker comment

I started Trade Paper AI after seeing how much export work still depends on copying the same buyer, product, cargo, and shipment information from one document to the next. The problem was not a lack of document templates—it was that the documents were disconnected. I wanted to build a practical workspace where an exporter enters reliable information once, carries it through the workflow, and can still review and edit every document before it is shared. This Founding Beta is the first step, and I am looking for the first export teams who want to shape it through real operational use.
